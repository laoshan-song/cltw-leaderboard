The dataset

Reading a whole page requires preserving its text through blur, reflection, occlusion and other real capture conditions. CLTW contains 14,183 field images of Chinese signs, exhibits and text-bearing surfaces across eight conditions. Normal and degraded views of the same content support evaluation of complete-page recognition and robustness.
